# Code of Conduct

NovelFoundry is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and practical community.
Participants must communicate in good faith and help keep discussions useful
for people with different backgrounds and levels of writing or engineering
experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and conduct that makes
participation unsafe or unreasonably hostile.

Report a concern privately to the maintainer through the project's security or
support channel. Reports are handled confidentially where possible. The
maintainer may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action
needed to protect the community.
