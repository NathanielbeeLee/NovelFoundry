# Creative Hub

Creative Hub is the conversational entry point for describing a writing intention and turning it into a focused task.

## Use it for

- Clarifying a premise or reader promise.
- Finding the next step in the novel completion path.
- Starting a director, planning, or chapter task.
- Reviewing the current task and its recommended action.

Creative Hub is connected to the novel workflow. It is not a second general-purpose chat state machine.
